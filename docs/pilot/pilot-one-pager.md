# Stingray Pilot Programme — Founding Vessels

**Passage & Performance Optimisation through AI Innovation · stingraymarinetechnology.com**

## What Stingray does

Stingray builds a physics-informed digital twin of your vessel and uses it to recommend the best passage — route, speed profile, and engine configuration — for whatever today's priorities are: fuel, schedule, guest comfort, or machinery care. Recommendations arrive as concrete setpoints on a bridge display; the chart and the conning decisions stay exactly where they are today. **Advisory only, by design — the captain always retains full authority.**

## What we're asking of a founding vessel

- Hosting a compact **logging kit** (NMEA 2000 gateway + processor, one-day install alongside, no yard period, no control connections, read-only by design) for one Mediterranean season.
- Normal operations — no change to how you run the boat. **~30+ sea days** of ordinary passages is what teaches the twin.
- One pre-install survey visit and a short commissioning check with your ETO/engineer.
- Candid feedback from the bridge as planning features come online during the season.

## What the vessel gets

- **Passage debriefs** from day one of the planner phase: fuel, timing, sea-state exposure vs plan — the evidence file that also feeds your SEEMP continuous-improvement record.
- The fitted **performance model of your hull** — fuel curves, seaway behaviour, fouling drift — yours to keep, useful well beyond Stingray.
- Founding-vessel terms on the commercial product, and a real say in what gets built.
- No fees either direction; hardware and installation at our cost.

## Privacy, straight up

Position and operational data are owner-sensitive. Per-vessel isolation; raw data is never shared, sold, or benchmarked against other vessels except as anonymised aggregates; the owner can end participation and take the data at any time. Full terms in the data agreement.

## Who we're looking for

1–2 displacement/semi-displacement motoryachts, 40–70 m, Mediterranean-based this season, with an engaged captain and chief engineer. Modern electronic engines (fuel-rate on the bus) preferred; flowmeters a bonus, not a requirement.

**See it live:** our planning demo runs on real forecasts and real geography — https://trigger-nav.github.io/stingray-demo/stingray_planner.html (synthetic vessel, advisory only, not for navigation).
