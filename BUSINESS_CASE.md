# Stingray — Market Size, Pricing & Business Case

**Version 0.1 · July 2026 · All figures are estimates for planning, not audited forecasts.**

## 1. Market size

| Segment | Vessels | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TAM — 30m+ operating fleet | ~6,170 | 5,260 motor / 915 sail (Aug 2025) |
| SAM — 40m+ (approx. 400GT boundary zone) | ~2,230 | Core regulatory + fuel-spend sweet spot |
| — of which 50m+ | ~1,000 | Highest fuel spend, strongest compliance pull |
| Order book | ~1,090 in build, avg 40.8m/551GT | Fleet is growing *larger* — tailwind |

The buyer channel is concentrated: the top ~10–15 management companies (Fraser, Burgess, Hill Robinson, Edmiston, IYC, etc.) manage a large share of the 40m+ fleet — a handful of enterprise deals unlocks hundreds of vessels.

## 2. Value anchor and SaaS pricing

Fuel spend: a 50m displacement yacht at moderate use (~400 cruising hrs/yr) spends **€250k+/yr on fuel**; larger or transoceanic/charter programmes reach **€400k–1M**. At Stingray's target 5–8% verified saving, per-vessel value is **€15k–60k/yr** in fuel alone, before wear reduction and compliance reporting value.

Pricing rule: capture roughly one-third of demonstrated value.

| Tier | Vessel | Price/yr | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lite | 30–40m | €6k | Lower fuel spend; funnel tier |
| Core | 40–60m | €12k | ~⅓ of typical fuel saving |
| Flagship | 60m+ | €24k | High fuel spend + CII/DCS exposure |
| Hardware + install (one-off) | all | €8k | Near cost; not a profit centre |

Blended ARPU assumption: **~€12k/yr**. Fleet discounts 10–20% for management companies. Expansion modules (anchor/hotel-load optimisation, charter itinerary planning, compliance pack) lift mature ARPU toward €18–20k.

## 3. Five-year sketch

| | Y1 | Y2 | Y3 | Y4 | Y5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vessels (cum.) | 10 (pilots) | 50 | 150 | 300 | 500 |
| ARR @ €12k blended | €0.1M | €0.6M | €1.8M | €3.6M | €6.0M |
| Hardware revenue | €0.1M | €0.3M | €0.8M | €1.2M | €1.6M |

- 500 vessels = ~8% of the 30m+ fleet, ~22% of 40m+ — ambitious but reachable through concentrated management-company channel.
- Software gross margin ~75–80%; hardware pass-through.
- Cost base: 6–10 engineers + 2–3 commercial (€1.5–2.5M/yr loaded), weather data licences, cloud, flag/class consultancy. **Breakeven around 200–250 vessels (Y4).**
- Funding implication: €3–5M to reach breakeven on this trajectory.

## 4. Honest assessment

- **This is a niche.** Yacht-only scope caps this at roughly a €5–15M ARR business. That's a solid, defensible company — but scale beyond that needs expansion paths.
- **Upside paths:** (a) adjacent commercial niches with the same profile — workboats, small ferries, expedition cruise; (b) becoming the *compliance data platform* for yacht management (higher strategic value than optimisation alone); (c) wear/usage data products for insurers and shipyards.
- **Key risks:** savings verification disputes (mitigate with counterfactual baseline methodology agreed upfront); slow yacht sales cycles; Starlink-era competitors porting commercial tools down-market; regulation timing slipping (Net-Zero Framework already delayed once).
- **Why now:** regulation direction is locked even if timing slips; the fleet is growing larger (order book avg 551GT); no incumbent owns this niche yet.
